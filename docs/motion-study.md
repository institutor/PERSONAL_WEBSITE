# Motion study — SAKAZUKI reference (56aff84d…mp4, 8.76s scroll capture)

Frame-by-frame analysis (4fps contact sheets + 8fps bursts at transitions + pixel sampling).
Every observed design/artistic choice, numbered. ✦ = adopted in our overhaul (different colorway).

## Structure & layout
1. ✦ The page is a stack of full-viewport **bands** in strictly alternating paper tones — warm bone and pure black. No gradients, no soft fades: transitions are hard horizontal edges that **rise** with scroll.
2. ✦ A **persistent chrome frame** never moves: top row = tiny uppercase nav links (left) + small outlined pill buttons with ↗ (right); bottom row = "(X) (IG) (OS)" (left), a **live localized clock** "JAPAN, 04:49:47" (center), "MINT ▶▶▶▶▶" (right). ~10px, recolors per band underneath.
3. ✦ Content pins to **extreme edges and corners**; the middle is mostly held empty for the display type. Body copy lives in narrow (~260px) columns of ~9-10px text with a bold first line acting as an inline heading.
4. ✦ Giant **section index numerals** ("03") parked in a corner at display scale.
5. ✦ Micro-labels in **parentheses** — "Philosophy" over "( life )" — a quiet, editorial labeling system.
6. ✦ At the top edge of a new band: a **three-part label row** spread full-width (JAPANESE | NFT ART | COLLECTION — left / center / right justified).

## Typography
7. ✦ ONE neutral grotesk family at extreme scale contrast: 10px labels against ~40vh display caps. Display is tracked tight (≈ -2%), CAPS for wordmarks, sentence case for statements ("SAKAZUKI deepens experience.").
8. ✦ **Broken-word display**: SAKAZUKI → "SAKA" / "ZUKI"; COLLECTIVE → "COLLE" / "CTIVE". Rows are horizontally offset against each other and deliberately **bleed off the viewport edges** (cropped letters are a feature).
9. ✦ Statement blocks: 3-5 words per line, ragged, mid-scale ("SAKAZUKI brings people together.").
10. ✦ The brand glyph **interrupts a wordmark**: BEN▼FITS (triangle replaces the E).

## Motion (everything is scroll-scrubbed; nothing autonomous except the clock)
11. ✦ Giant words **reveal bottom-up from behind the band edge / divider strip** — a masked rise, not a fade.
12. ✦ Display rows have **scroll-linked horizontal travel**: as you scroll, the top row drifts one way and the bottom row the other (differential rates — a scrubbed, opposing marquee). COLLE slides right while CTIVE holds/counters.
13. ✦ Photos/panels **collapse or scale** as you scroll away from them (the Philosophy photo shrinks toward its top edge).
14. ✦ Small text blocks translate slightly slower than their band (subtle parallax layering, 2-3 depths).
15. ✦ The scrub feel is mechanical and precise — motion is position, not easing; the site reads as an instrument.

## Shapes & ornament
16. ✦ **The signature ornament: a repeating sawtooth strip of solid triangles (▼▼▼▼)** in poster red, used as baseline/divider bands under display rows — the site's identity in one element.
17. ✦ One brand glyph (▼) recycled *everywhere*: tiny floating page marker between sections, pinned badge on photos, giant collage-filled hero shape, notch cut into panel tops, word interruption, chevrons of MINT ▶▶▶▶▶.
18. ✦ **Random small shapes** pinned at deterministic spots (red hearts, tiny triangles, blue accents) — playful noise against the rigid grid, some drifting on scroll.
19. ✦ Giant **rounded rectangle panel** in the accent color, with a triangle **notch** cut into its top edge; holds a photo card + copy (the "Benefits" card).
20. Art/texture appears ONLY inside shape masks (the patchwork collage lives inside the giant triangle) — imagery never floats free. (We keep this principle; our "texture" is the pixel-dissolve.)

## Color (pixel-sampled)
21. Bone/cream ≈ #E5DED2 (panel edge #DED3CC), pure #000000 black, poster red **#C10B22**, ink near-black on cream. Photos warm amber. Exactly ONE accent.
    ✦ Our colorway: same bone + black skeleton, accent swapped to **ultramarine #1E32E8** ("voltage"), ink #101015.

## Voice & chrome details

23. ✦ Social links written "(X) (IG) (OS)" → ours: "(GH) (IN) (@)".
24. ✦ CTA as an oversized wordmark with stacked chevrons: MINT ▶▶▶▶▶ → ours: RESUME ▶▶▶▶▶ (PDF download), repeated giant in the contact band.
25. ✦ Tiny outlined pill buttons with ↗ for external links.

## Mapping to the overhaul
- Hero (per Jiewen's reference): "I'm" massive top-left, "JIEWEN" massive bottom-right (bigger than before, bleeding off the right edge), separated by a full-width **animated sawtooth divider strip** (slow continuous marquee, velocity couples to scroll).
